\section{Research Questions}

Our empirical evaluation is guided by the following overarching question: \textit{When heterogeneous AI agents become interoperable, what persistent state, evidence, authority, and governance primitives are required to preserve continuity?} 

We decompose this into three specific, empirically testable research questions:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{RQ1 (Work-State Transfer Efficiency):} Does structured metadata transfer (the `ExperimentalWorkTransfer` schema) significantly reduce receiver agent rework, token consumption, and failure rates compared to raw transcript replay during a single-hop task handoff?
    \item \textbf{RQ2 (Project Intelligence Continuity):} Does maintaining a longitudinal, verified project intelligence layer across multiple sessions reduce the incidence of architectural contradictions and hallucinated constraints compared to fresh, memory-less sessions?
    \item \textbf{RQ3 (Reconciliation and Governance):} How does the enforcement of a strict commitment lifecycle (differentiating between \textit{proposed}, \textit{decided}, \textit{implemented}, \textit{verified}, and \textit{superseded} states) impact the frequency of unauthorized or stale agent actions?
\end{itemize}
