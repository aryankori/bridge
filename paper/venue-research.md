# Venue Research

This document outlines realistic academic publishing venues for the empirical research paper on Bridge and Agentic Project Truth.

## 1. Primary Targets (Software Engineering)

### ASE 2026/2027 (IEEE/ACM International Conference on Automated Software Engineering)
- **Status:** Prime venue for tooling, multi-agent frameworks, and automation.
- **Paper Type:** Research Paper (empirical / tooling).
- **Page Limit:** 10 pages + 2 pages references (ACM format).
- **Fit:** HIGH. ASE explicitly focuses on automating software engineering processes. A paper evaluating the failure boundaries of AI agents during work-transfer aligns perfectly.

### ICSE 2027 (International Conference on Software Engineering)
- **Status:** The premier software engineering conference.
- **Paper Type:** Technical Track.
- **Page Limit:** 10 pages + references.
- **Fit:** HIGH, but highly competitive. Requires a flawless empirical evaluation (large $n$, strict controls) to prove the thesis.

### FSE 2026/2027 (ACM International Conference on the Foundations of Software Engineering)
- **Status:** Premier SE conference alongside ICSE.
- **Fit:** HIGH. Strong focus on empirical studies of developer workflows and tools.

## 2. Secondary Targets (AI & Systems)

### CAIN 2027 (Conf. on AI Engineering - Software Engineering for AI)
- **Fit:** MEDIUM. Usually focuses on engineering AI systems rather than AI for SE, but agentic pipelines blur this line.

### AAMAS 2027 (Intl. Conf. on Autonomous Agents and Multiagent Systems)
- **Fit:** MEDIUM. Focuses heavily on the theoretical underpinnings of agent interaction (Commitments, norms, BDI models). If the paper leans heavily into the theoretical *reconciliation and commitment control* framework, this is a strong theoretical fit.

## 3. Journals

### IEEE Software
- **Status:** Practitioner-oriented journal.
- **Fit:** HIGH for a pragmatic, systems-level paper explaining *why* current agent memory fails and how Project Truth solves it. Shorter format, less rigid empirical requirements than ICSE/ASE.

### ACM TOSEM (Transactions on Software Engineering and Methodology)
- **Fit:** HIGH, but very slow review cycle. Good for an extended version of an ASE/ICSE paper.

## 4. Immediate Strategy

1. **Preprint:** Publish to **arXiv** immediately after the empirical program concludes and the paper is finalized. This establishes primacy on the "Project Truth vs. Agent Memory" distinction.
2. **Submission Target:** Target **ASE 2026/2027** as the primary empirical venue, utilizing the `acmart` LaTeX class.
